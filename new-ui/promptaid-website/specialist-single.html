About

A physiotherapy practice in Durban North treating back and neck pain, sports injuries and post-operative rehabilitation, including knee and hip replacements. Six-session blocks are priced below the single-session rate and can be claimed as one authorisation from most schemes. Home visits are available for patients who cannot travel.
