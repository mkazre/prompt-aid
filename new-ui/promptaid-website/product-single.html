What's included

A full blood count with differential, serum ferritin, serum iron, transferrin saturation and total iron-binding capacity. Used to investigate fatigue, suspected iron-deficiency anaemia and chronic inflammation.

Turnaround
48 hours
Sample
Venous blood, 2 tubes
Fasting
Not required
Referral
Optional

Preparation

  • No fasting required for this panel.
  • Stop iron supplements 24 hours before the draw — they skew serum iron.
  • Bring your ID and medical aid card to the collection centre.
  • Results load to your record within 48 hours and go to your referring doctor automatically.
