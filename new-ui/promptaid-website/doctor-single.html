About

Dr Mokoena runs a family practice at Rosebank Medical Suites with a special interest in chronic disease management — hypertension, type 2 diabetes and HIV. She consults in person Monday to Friday and by video on Tuesday and Thursday evenings. New patients are welcome and same-week appointments are usually available.
