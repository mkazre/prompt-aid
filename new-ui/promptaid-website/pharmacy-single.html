About

An independent dispensing pharmacy in Rosebank filling acute and chronic scripts, with same-day delivery across the northern suburbs and collection from the counter. Scripts uploaded through Prompt Aid are reviewed by a pharmacist before the order is released, usually within the hour during trading times.
